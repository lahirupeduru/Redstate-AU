RED STATE — website
====================

FILES
  index.html        The whole website (edit text/prices here).
  images/           Put your photos here.

GO LIVE ON HOSTINGER
  1. Log in to Hostinger -> hPanel -> your domain -> "File Manager".
  2. Open the public_html folder.
  3. Upload index.html and the whole images folder into public_html.
  4. Visit your domain. Done.
  (If hosting elsewhere / FTP, upload the same files to the web root.)

ADD PHOTOS
  Drop a file named exactly:  images/hero.jpg   (a good main photo of work/crew)
  It fills the big picture on the homepage. Portrait (taller than wide) looks best.
  Social share preview: add images/og-cover.jpg (1200x630px).
  If a photo is missing the site still looks fine — it shows a placeholder.

EDIT TEXT OR PRICES
  Open index.html in any text editor and change the words. Prices live in the
  "PACKAGES" section. Phone numbers/email are in the "CONTACT" section and the
  <head> at the top.

CONTACT FORM
  Right now "Send request" opens the visitor's email app addressed to
  hello@redstate.com.au. That works everywhere with no setup.
  Want messages delivered to your inbox automatically instead?
    - Sign up free at https://web3forms.com and get an Access Key.
    - Ask to switch the form to Web3Forms and paste the key in.
